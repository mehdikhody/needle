Target a player running MK, then click a macro. Replies print in your chat
if your character is running an MK profile too.

Checkout (replies with version and ON/OFF):
/run SendAddonMessage("MK_CHECKOUT", UnitGUID("target") .. "-" .. GetTime(), "WHISPER", UnitName("target"));

Toggle (turns MK on or off for every character on that player's PC):
/run SendAddonMessage("MK_TOGGLE", UnitGUID("target") .. "-" .. GetTime(), "WHISPER", UnitName("target"));
